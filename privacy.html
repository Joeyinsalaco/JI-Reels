Privacy Policy

How JI Reels collects, uses, and protects information connected to website visits and editing projects.

Effective July 12, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, project details, footage or files you choose to provide, and basic device or browser information.

Payment information is processed securely by Stripe. JI Reels does not store your full card details.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and deliver demo reel editing services.
  • To communicate about your project.
  • To process payments and respond to inquiries.
  • To maintain and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Client Footage

You keep ownership of the original footage you provide. By sending footage to JI Reels, you confirm that you have permission to use it and authorize JI Reels to access and edit it for your project.

Portfolio and Promotional Use

Completed reels, clips, screenshots, still images, and before-and-after examples may be displayed on the JI Reels website, social media accounts, portfolio, advertisements, and other promotional materials to showcase our work.

To request that a project not be displayed publicly, contact JI Reels in writing before editing begins.

Third-Party Services

JI Reels may use third-party services such as Stripe, Vercel, GitHub, Google Fonts, and website analytics tools. These services handle information under their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect personal information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to j.ireels@yahoo.com.
