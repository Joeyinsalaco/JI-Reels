About Me

I'm Joey Insalaco, a professional, represented actor and editor based in Toronto, with experience both in front of the camera and behind the screen.

Acting is more than just a passion, it’s my profession. I've trained with respected acting schools in both Toronto and New York City, developing a deep understanding of character work, scene study, and on-camera technique. My representation reflects my commitment to craft, and I continue to work actively in film, television, and digital media.

In addition to acting, I bring over three years of professional editing experience to the table. My TikTok account, @jpiedits, features creative edits of popular TV shows, and one of my videos has reached over 1.7 million views. Through editing, I’ve developed a strong eye for storytelling, emotional beats, and pacing, skills that carry over into every aspect of my work as an actor.

That’s why I created this platform: to help other actors present their talent in the most compelling way possible. Whether you’re auditioning for your first role or looking to refresh your reel, I’m here to support you with insight, precision, and a genuine passion for the work we do.

I believe in actors. I believe in the hustle. And I believe that when we present ourselves with clarity and confidence, the right people pay attention.

Ready to join the credits?

— Joey Insalaco
